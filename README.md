# Problem Background
In the banking industry, understanding customer demographics is importance for developing effective marketing strategies and financial products. Customers with different age may have different financial needs, spending habits, and saving behaviors

For example, young customers may prioritize in building savings & gaining financial foundations, while order customers may prioritizing in financial stability, wealth preservation & long-term financial planning

These differences is an opportunity for banks to better understand their customers, and create different campaign and strategies towards different age groups

# Data
Data was taken from [UCI Machine Learning](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/222/bank+marketing), data is related with direct marketing campaigns of a portugese banking institution, containing `45211 Rows` and `16 Columns`. This Data has an classification goal for data scientist where they need to predict if the client will subscribe a term deposit
